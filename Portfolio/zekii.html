(04) Process
How I build

    
    

      
01
Understand
What the business does, who uses it and what it must achieve. Nothing is built before this is clear.

      
02
Architect
Pick the simplest structure that will last. Fast, secure and easy to extend later.

      
03
Build
Write it cleanly, test it on real phones, and show progress early so you can steer.

      
04
Ship & look after
Launch it, watch how it behaves, and keep improving. Long-term thinking, not one-off work.
