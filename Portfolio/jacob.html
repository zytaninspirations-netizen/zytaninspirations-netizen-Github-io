(04) Approach

How a film gets made

01

Observe

Spend time with people and places until they forget the camera is there. The honest moments arrive on their own schedule.

02

Shape

Observation gives the material. Docudrama and careful editing give it a structure, so a real story plays like cinema.

03

Finish

Grade, sound and delivery handled with care, whether it is a documentary, a café promo or an advertising film.
