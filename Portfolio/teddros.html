(05) Approach

How I shoot

01

Look first

Spend time with the place and the people before lifting the camera. The best frame is usually the one you waited for.

02

Keep it honest

Natural light, real expressions, minimal staging. The picture should feel like the moment, not a copy of it.

03

Deliver clean

Edited consistently, sized for where it will live, and handed over on time, whether for a café menu or a campaign.
