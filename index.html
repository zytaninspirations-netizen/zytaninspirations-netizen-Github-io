    (10) FAQ

    Good to know

    How do I order a service?

    Use the project builder above: choose your services, tell us about your business and needs. The site prepares a message that you review and send on WhatsApp or Telegram.

    Can I see samples of your videos and websites?

    Yes. The Work section has café promotions, films, advertising videos and websites. Tap any card with a play button to watch, or the arrow to visit the site.

    What do you need to make my café's digital menu?

    Your items, prices, photos and logo. We design the menu in your brand style and give you a QR code to print and place on tables, cups or doors.

    Do you work with businesses other than restaurants?

    Yes. We offer digital marketing, advertising videos, websites and systems for many kinds of businesses.

    Does the website send my message by itself?

    No. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or Telegram, so you stay in full control of what we receive.

    What is the fastest way to reach you?

    WhatsApp or phone on +251 923 231 497, or Telegram @ZAYTANS.
